The Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is a landlocked country in Southern Africa. It is surrounded by South Africa and is known for its high-altitude landscape, mountain communities and distinctive cultural identity.

The country is often called the Kingdom in the Sky because much of its land lies at high elevation. Its scenery includes mountains, rivers, valleys and waterfalls.

Visitors can learn about Basotho traditions, explore local crafts and enjoy outdoor activities in different parts of the country.
